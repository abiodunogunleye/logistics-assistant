# Northbank Logistics — SOP: Picking Errors

**Document ID:** SOP-PE-01
**Version:** 1.1
**Owner:** Warehouse Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-06

## Purpose
Defines how to report, correct, and prevent picking errors — mis-picks, wrong
quantities, and wrong labels — so they are caught before dispatch and don't
repeat.

## Scope
Applies to all warehouse operatives, First Line Managers (FLMs), and Senior
FLMs involved in order picking at any Northbank Logistics site.

## Error Types

### Mis-pick (wrong item)
The SKU placed in the tote does not match the SKU on the pick list.

### Wrong quantity
The quantity picked is higher or lower than the quantity on the pick list.

### Wrong label
The shipping or location label applied does not match the order, carton
contents, or bin location.

## Procedure

### 1. Reporting
1. Stop picking that order line as soon as an error is found or suspected.
2. Do not try to quietly "fix and continue" — log it first.
3. Scan or note: order number, SKU expected, SKU/quantity found, and bin
   location.
4. Log the error in the incident system (or the paper incident log if the
   system is down) within 5 minutes of discovery.
5. Tell your FLM straight away for any error involving a safety-critical
   item, a high-value SKU, or a carton already sealed.

### 2. Correcting
1. Return any wrongly picked item to its correct bin location — do not leave
   it in the tote or on the pack bench.
2. Re-pick the correct SKU and quantity from the pick list, scanning both the
   location and item barcodes.
3. If the carton is already sealed, open it, correct the contents, and
   re-label if the carton was marked.
4. Get a second person (your FLM or a buddy picker) to verify the correction
   before the order moves on to packing.
5. Update the incident log with how the error was corrected and who verified
   it.

### 3. Preventing Recurrence
1. Check whether the error was caused by a mislabelled or empty bin — report
   bin issues separately so stock accuracy stays correct.
2. Where RF scanning is available, always scan rather than visually
   confirm — visual-only picks are a leading cause of mis-picks.
3. FLMs review the incident log at the end of each shift and flag any SKU or
   picker with repeated errors to the Senior FLM for retraining or a bin
   location review.
4. Persistent location issues (e.g. two SKUs sharing a bin) should be raised
   by the FLM as a stock layout change request, not worked around.

## Reporting / Escalation
- Any error on a safety-critical or high-value SKU: notify your FLM
  immediately, not at end of shift.
- Three or more errors by the same picker in one shift: the FLM pulls them
  from picking for a refresher before they continue, and informs the Senior
  FLM.
- Repeated errors on the same SKU or bin across different pickers: the FLM
  raises a stock layout change request with the Senior FLM.
- If your FLM is unavailable, go to the Senior FLM.
