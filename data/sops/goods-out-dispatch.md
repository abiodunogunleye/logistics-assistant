# Northbank Logistics — SOP: Goods-Out & Dispatch

**Document ID:** SOP-GO-01
**Version:** 1.1
**Owner:** Warehouse Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-06

## Purpose
Defines the standard process for picking, packing, labelling, loading, and final
checks on outbound orders, to ensure the right goods reach the right customer in
good condition and on time.

## Scope
Applies to all warehouse operatives working goods-out shifts, and the First
Line Managers (FLMs), Senior FLMs, and Shift Managers who oversee them, at any
Northbank Logistics site. Covers manual and RF-scanner-assisted picking.

## Procedure

### 1. Picking
1. Pick only against a released pick list — never a hand-written note.
2. Scan the location barcode, then the item barcode, before removing stock.
3. If the scanned SKU does not match the pick list, stop and follow SOP-PE-01
   (Picking Errors) before continuing.
4. Pick the exact quantity shown on the pick list. Do not round up or down to
   a "convenient" case size without FLM sign-off.
5. Place picked items in a tote or cage labelled with the order number.

### 2. Packing
1. Check picked items against the pick list line by line before sealing any
   carton.
2. Use the smallest carton size that fits the order without crushing stock.
3. Add void fill for fragile or loose items.
4. Include a packing slip inside every carton.
5. Do not pack damaged stock — remove it and follow SOP-DG-01 — Damaged
   Goods Handling.

### 3. Labelling
1. Generate the shipping label from the order management system only — never
   hand-write a label for a live order.
2. Match the label's order number to the packing slip inside the carton before
   applying it.
3. Apply one label per carton, placed flat on the largest face, with no
   creases over the barcode.
4. For multi-carton orders, label each carton "Box X of Y".

### 4. Loading
1. Stage packed cartons by carrier and route in the marked dispatch bay —
   never block a fire exit or emergency lane.
2. Scan each carton onto the outbound manifest as it is loaded.
3. Load heaviest cartons first and nearest the vehicle bulkhead.
4. Do not load any carton that fails a label or packing-slip check — set it
   aside and tell your FLM.

### 5. Dispatch Checks
1. Confirm the scanned carton count on the manifest matches the physical
   count on the vehicle before it leaves.
2. Get the driver's signature (or electronic POD scan) confirming collection.
3. File or digitally archive the signed manifest the same day.
4. Report any shortage, mislabelled carton, or late vehicle to your FLM
   immediately.

## Reporting / Escalation
- Mis-picks or quantity discrepancies: follow SOP-PE-01.
- Damaged stock found during packing: follow SOP-DG-01 before continuing.
- Missed carrier collection: notify your FLM within 15 minutes. If the order
  cannot be rebooked for same-day dispatch, the FLM escalates to the Senior
  FLM.
- If your FLM is unavailable, go to the Senior FLM.
