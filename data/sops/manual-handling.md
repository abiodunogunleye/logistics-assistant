# Northbank Logistics — SOP: Manual Handling

**Document ID:** SOP-MH-01
**Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Warehouse Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-06

## Purpose
Defines safe manual handling practice for warehouse operatives, to reduce
the risk of injury when lifting, carrying, or moving stock.

## Scope
Applies to all warehouse operatives at any Northbank Logistics site who
lift, carry, push, or pull loads as part of their role.

## Procedure

### 1. Before Lifting
1. Assess the load's weight, size, and shape before attempting to move it.
   Check the carton label for weight where marked.
2. If a load is, or looks like it could be, over 20kg for one person, use a
   team lift or mechanical aid instead.
3. Clear the path of trip hazards and confirm there is space to set the load
   down at the destination before picking it up.

### 2. Lifting Technique
1. Stand close to the load with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Bend at the knees, not the back, keeping the load close to the body.
3. Get a secure grip before lifting and keep the back straight through the
   lift.
4. Lift smoothly — do not jerk or twist while holding the load. Turn with
   your feet, not your spine.

### 3. Team Lifts
1. Agree who is calling the lift before starting.
2. Lift, move, and set down in time with each other on the caller's count.
3. Use a trolley, pallet truck, or hoist instead of a team lift wherever one
   is available and suitable.

### 4. Equipment Use
1. Only operators trained and signed off on a piece of equipment (pallet
   truck, stacker, etc.) may use it unsupervised.
2. Do a quick visual check for defects before each use. The full pre-use
   check is done at the start of each shift and recorded on the Pre-Use
   Equipment Checklist, per SOP-EQ-01.
3. Report faults to your FLM rather than working around them.

### 5. Reporting Strain or Injury
1. Report any strain, pain, or injury to your FLM or a first aider
   immediately, however minor it seems. Make sure your FLM knows, even if a
   first aider has dealt with it.
2. Log the incident in the incident system (or paper log if the system is
   down) the same shift, per SOP-IR-01.
3. Do not continue manual handling duties after an injury until cleared by
   your FLM.

## Reporting / Escalation
- Any injury, however minor: report to your FLM or a first aider
  immediately, and log per SOP-IR-01.
- Repeated strain reports from the same task or area: the FLM raises it with
  the Senior FLM as a task redesign or equipment request.
- If your FLM is unavailable, go to the Senior FLM.
