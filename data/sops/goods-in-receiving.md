# Northbank Logistics — SOP: Goods-In & Receiving

**Document ID:** SOP-GI-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Warehouse Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

## Purpose
Defines the standard process for unloading, checking, and putting away
inbound deliveries, so stock records match what has physically arrived.

## Scope
Applies to all warehouse operatives working goods-in shifts at any Northbank
Logistics site, covering supplier deliveries and inter-site transfers.

## Procedure

### 1. Unloading
1. Check the vehicle and trailer seal number against the delivery note
   before breaking the seal, where a seal is used.
2. Visually inspect pallets for crushed, leaning, or wet cartons before
   unloading them.
3. Unload onto the marked goods-in staging area only — never directly onto
   a pick face.

### 2. Checking Against PO / ASN
1. Match the delivery note or advance shipping notice (ASN) to an open
   purchase order before accepting the delivery.
2. Scan or count each SKU and quantity against the PO/ASN line by line.
3. If a quantity is short, over, or a SKU is not on the PO, do not guess —
   log it as a discrepancy per Section 4.
4. Reject any delivery with no matching PO, pending supervisor approval.

### 3. Quality Check
1. Open a sample carton per pallet (or per SOP-DG-01 trigger) to check for
   damage, expiry date, or contamination.
2. If damage is found, follow SOP-DG-01 — Damaged Goods Handling before
   continuing to putaway.
3. Confirm the SKU on the outer carton matches the SKU on the product label
   inside.

### 4. Discrepancy Handling
1. Log any quantity mismatch, wrong SKU, or damaged pallet in the incident
   system the same shift.
2. Hold the affected pallet in goods-in staging until a supervisor confirms
   how to proceed — do not put away stock with an open discrepancy.
3. Notify the buyer or supplier contact for repeated or high-value
   discrepancies.

### 5. Putaway
1. Putaway only stock that has passed checking and quality review.
2. Scan the item barcode and the destination bin barcode for every putaway
   to keep location records accurate.
3. Do not split a SKU across more bins than necessary — consolidate into an
   existing partial bin first if space allows.

## Reporting / Escalation
- Rejected deliveries or large discrepancies: notify the shift supervisor
  immediately.
- Damaged pallets found on arrival: follow SOP-DG-01.
