# Northbank Logistics — SOP: Damaged Goods Handling

**Document ID:** SOP-DG-01
**Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** Warehouse Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-06

## Purpose
Defines how to identify, quarantine, report, and resolve damaged stock found
anywhere in the warehouse, so damaged items never reach a customer and losses
are recorded accurately.

## Scope
Applies to all warehouse operatives, First Line Managers (FLMs), Senior FLMs,
and Shift Managers at any Northbank Logistics site, covering damage found
during goods-in, putaway, picking, packing, or routine stock checks.

## Procedure

### 1. Identifying Damage
1. Treat any item with a torn, crushed, wet, or opened outer as damaged until
   inspected, even if the contents look fine.
2. Check for leaking, expired, or visibly contaminated product on food,
   chemical, or liquid SKUs.
3. If unsure whether an item counts as damaged, treat it as damaged and ask
   your FLM rather than guess.

### 2. Quarantine
1. Remove the damaged item from the pick face or receiving area immediately.
2. Move it to the designated damaged-goods quarantine area — never leave it
   in a live bin, on the pack bench, or in goods-in staging.
3. At goods-in, only the damaged item goes to quarantine. The rest of its
   pallet is held in goods-in staging until the discrepancy is resolved, per
   SOP-GI-01 Section 4.
4. Attach a damaged-goods tag stating SKU, quantity, date, and who found it.

### 3. Reporting
1. Log the damage in the incident system (or paper log if the system is
   down) within 15 minutes of discovery.
2. Record SKU, quantity, damage type, and where it was found.
3. Take a photo of the damage where the system supports photo attachments.
4. Tell your FLM straight away for high-value items (see SOP-IR-01
   Definitions) or damage affecting more than one unit of the same batch.

### 4. Resolution
1. The FLM decides whether the item is written off, returned to supplier,
   or downgraded for internal use, per current stock policy.
2. Write-offs of high-value items (see SOP-IR-01 Definitions), or of a whole
   batch, need Senior FLM approval before stock records are changed.
3. Update stock records to remove written-off quantity from available stock
   the same day.
4. File supplier-fault damage against the relevant purchase order for
   recharge or replacement.

### 5. Prevention
1. If damage is repeatedly linked to a specific racking location, handling
   method, or supplier packaging, raise it with your FLM rather than logging
   it as a one-off each time. The FLM passes it to the Senior FLM as a
   layout or packaging issue.

## Reporting / Escalation
- Any damage affecting food safety or chemical containment: notify your FLM
  immediately, not at end of shift. The FLM informs the Shift Manager
  straight away.
- Repeated damage from the same supplier: the FLM raises it with the Senior
  FLM, who follows up with the supplier and informs the Shift Manager.
- If your FLM is unavailable, go to the Senior FLM.
